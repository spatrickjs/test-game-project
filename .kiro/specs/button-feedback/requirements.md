# Gameplay button feedback

- Holding a gameplay button darkens and compresses its artwork while its touch rectangle stays stable.
- Selected crops and tools display a gold outline and checkmark, including selection updates made without signals.
- Release, leaving the button, disabling, hiding, pausing, and loss of application focus clear held feedback.
- Disabled buttons are visibly dimmed.
- Crops form an evenly spaced right column. Field actions align below them. Pause stays above the crop column.
- Individual button rectangles remain editable in the scene.
