# Adaptive tile inspector

- Left-side tiles open the inspector on their right and right-side tiles on their left.
- Placement follows the camera, stays inside the safe screen bounds, and avoids occupied HUD rectangles where room permits.
- Narrow screens use a compact bottom panel with scrolling.
- Cream, bronze, brown, and muted green styling matches the farm controls without image assets.
- Rounded, colored bars show labeled percentages and animate their visual fill.
- Empty tiles, invalid values, missing crop definitions, and long disease names remain readable.
